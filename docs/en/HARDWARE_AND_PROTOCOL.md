# Hardware and protocol notes — Radioactive@Home

Author: **Marcin Kowalik <mkowalik@agh.edu.pl>**

Validated device: Microchip Radioactive@Home GRS `04d8:f6fe`, revision 3.01. The legacy RADAC application works in standalone mode on 32-bit Trixie and reports a 240 s sample interval and a 21-sample block. Backlight and buzzer preferences are supported; GM supply and counter-reset capabilities are deliberately not automated by this project.
